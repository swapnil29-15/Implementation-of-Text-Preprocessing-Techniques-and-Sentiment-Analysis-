## Brief Summary of Assignment 4

Title: Implementation of Text Preprocessing Techniques and Sentiment Analysis Using R Programming

The objective of this assignment is to analyse Twitter data related to demonetisation using R programming. It focuses on cleaning unstructured text data, identifying frequently used words and understanding the sentiment expressed by users.

### Work performed in this assignment

1\. Dataset Import: Imported the Demonetisation Twitter dataset containing 15,844 tweets and 16 columns.

2\. Text Preprocessing: Converted text into lowercase and removed punctuation, numbers, special characters and extra spaces.

3\. Stop-word Removal: Removed commonly used English words such as "is", "the", "and" and "are".

4\. Tokenization: Divided tweets into individual words called tokens.

5\. Stemming: Reduced words to their root forms by removing word endings.

6\. Lemmatization: Converted words into their meaningful dictionary base forms.

7\. Word Frequency Analysis: Identified the most frequently used words, such as demonetization, Modi, India, people, RBI and bank.

8\. Sentiment Analysis: Used the Bing Sentiment Lexicon to analyse positive and negative words in tweets.

9\. Sentiment Classification: Classified tweets into three categories: Positive, Negative and Neutral.

10\. Data Visualization: Created bar charts to represent frequently used words and sentiment distribution.
